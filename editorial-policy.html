Who the byline is

The byline on the trade guides is the Mortar Smash editorial team. That name is the site's brand byline. It is not a named bricklayer, and it is not an engineer's or an architect's licence. A guide that carries the byline is general information. It is not a structural design, a specification, or a reading of the code that governs a particular job. Where a page mentions load, wall ties, flashing or a building regulation, it cites the note or the code and says to check the documents for that job. See the disclaimer.

What a number has to come from

A strength, ratio, spacing or pigment limit stays on a page only when a primary source states it. The source is linked in the sentence and again in the Sources list at the bottom of the guide. The sources we use are:

  • Brick Industry Association Technical Notes, the PDF on gobrick.com, cited by note number and the date printed on the note (for example Technical Note 8, March 2020, and Technical Note 7B, November 2017).
  • ASTM standards by their designation, linked to the ASTM store page, when a guide names a US classification such as C270, C144, C33, C91, C979, C1714 or C476. The store page is the identifier. We do not reprint a paywalled standard. When a figure is taken from a BIA note that itself cites ASTM, the sentence says so, and it names the edition the note used.
  • National Park Service Preservation Brief 2 (October 1998) for historic repointing.
  • A UK document only on a guide that is about UK practice, linked to the publisher (for example a BSI page, the Brick Development Association, or an Approved Document).
  • A manufacturer's data sheet only for what that sheet says about its own product. A QUIKRETE proportion is a QUIKRETE example. It is not treated as a code.

Illustrative ratios are labelled illustrative in the sentence. We do not invent a "typical" figure, a quote, or a test result the source does not give. If a link moves, the claim comes off the page or the link is replaced with the document we actually re-read.

When a page is checked

Each guide carries the date it was published. When we change the substance, the note at the bottom says when the sources were re-read. That date is a check date, not a new publish date. The Article markup keeps the original datePublished.

Corrections

If a figure does not match the source, use the contact form or email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com. We correct the page. We do not leave a disputed number up while we think about it.

Images

Article text and the bond diagrams are original to this Site. Illustrative photographs are labelled AI-generated in the caption under the image, and that caption links to the image policy. Those pictures show the idea of a mix or a repair. They are not a specific product, brand or job site.
