How long is mixed mortar good for?

Plain-English mortar guidance for bricklayers and site work

Short answer

Use cement-lime mortar within 2½ hours of initial mixing, or within 2 hours in hot weather. ASTM C270 sets that window, as explained in BIA Technical Note 8. Inside it, adding a little water to replace what has evaporated (retempering) is allowed and often helps bond. Once mortar has started to set, throw it away.

Retempering: when it is fine

Mortar on the board stiffens for two reasons: water evaporates, and the cement starts to hydrate. Only the first can be fixed by adding water. BIA Technical Note 8 says retempering to replace evaporated water should be encouraged when needed. Compressive strength may drop slightly and colour may lighten, but bond strength can suffer more if stiff mortar is not retempered.

BIA also notes that specifications which demand the minimum water and ban retempering are mistaken for brickwork. Mortar mixed with the maximum water consistent with workability gives the best bond.

When not to retemper

  • After the time limit. Past 2½ hours (2 hours in hot weather) the mortar is beginning to set. Water will make it sloppy without restoring it.
  • Coloured mortar. BIA recommends not retempering pigmented mortar because the extra water dilutes the colour. Mix smaller batches instead. See coloured mortar.
  • Historic repointing mortar. The National Park Service brief on repointing says repointing mortar should not be retempered, and once portland cement is in the mix it should be used within about 30 minutes to 1½ hours.

Grout is different

Masonry grout, the fluid fill for reinforced cores, has its own shorter window. BIA Technical Note 1 notes the TMS Specification requires grout to be placed within 1½ hours of mixing. See mortar vs grout vs concrete.

Simple board routine

  1. Note the time each batch is mixed. A marker on the mixer works.
  2. Size batches so each is used in well under the limit. In heat, go smaller.
  3. When mortar stiffens from drying, add a little water and remix on the board.
  4. When it stiffens and will not come back to a smooth, plastic mix, discard it.

Batch size help: our mortar calculator estimates mortar per 100 or 1,000 bricks.

How long mortar stays usable FAQ

How long can mortar sit before it goes off?

Use it within 2½ hours of initial mixing under ASTM C270, or 2 hours in hot weather. Mortar that has begun to set should be discarded.

Can you add water to mortar that has started to dry?

Yes, within the time limit. Adding water to replace evaporation, called retempering, is permitted and usually improves bond. It is not recommended for coloured mortar or historic repointing mortar.

Does retempering weaken mortar?

Compressive strength may be slightly lower and colour may lighten, but BIA notes bond strength may be lower if stiff mortar is used without retempering.

Can I keep mixed mortar overnight?

Not cement based mortar; it will have set. Lime putty and sand mixes without cement are different and can be stored airtight, as the NPS repointing brief describes.
