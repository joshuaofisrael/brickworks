Short answer

Mortar is the stiff, sticky paste that beds and bonds brick or block. Masonry grout is a fluid mix poured into cores and cavities to bond reinforcement. Concrete is a structural material with coarse aggregate. They are not interchangeable: mortar is made to stay workable on a trowel, grout to flow (BIA notes a slump of at least 8 in), and concrete to carry load on its own.

Why mortar is not just weak concrete

BIA Technical Note 8 calls flexural bond strength perhaps the most important physical property of hardened mortar; compressive strength is not the main goal. Lime and fine sand give it workability and water retention so it spreads, sticks, and seals against the brick. Using a very strong, stiff mix to "make it stronger" often gives worse bond and a joint harder than the brick. Mortar types are explained in Types N, S, M and O.

Grout is not tile grout

Masonry grout and tile grout share a name only. Masonry grout is a structural fill for reinforced brick and block walls. Its high water content is deliberate: the dry masonry absorbs some water, which BIA notes helps it still reach adequate strength. In freezing weather that same water makes grout vulnerable, which is why cold weather grout must be heated to 70 °F to 120 °F at mixing (see cold weather bricklaying).

Sand matters

Mortar uses fine masonry sand (ASTM C144). Concrete uses concrete sand (ASTM C33) plus stone. Our building sand vs sharp sand guide covers the difference.

Mortar vs grout vs concrete FAQ

Can I use concrete mix instead of mortar for bricks?

No. Concrete mix contains coarse aggregate and lacks lime, so it does not spread, stick or seal joints like mortar. Use a mortar mix to ASTM C270.

Can I use mortar as grout?

Not as a substitute. Masonry grout must be fluid enough to fill cores completely around reinforcement; mortar is too stiff and would leave voids.

What is stronger, mortar or concrete?

Concrete is usually much stronger in compression, but strength is the wrong measure for mortar. Mortar is designed for bond and workability.

How long do I have to use masonry grout?

The TMS Specification requires grout to be placed within 1½ hours of mixing.
