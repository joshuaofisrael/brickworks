Which images are AI-generated

Original diagrams on the trade guides, including the brick-selection, frost-exposure and bond-pattern drawings, were made for this site. They are not photographs and are not AI-generated. Each diagram is credited in its caption and is an illustration, not a construction detail.

Illustrative photographs on the mortar articles are AI-generated. Each of those figures has a caption that includes the words “AI-generated” and a link to this page. That includes the photos on mortar types N, S, M, and O, lime vs cement mortar, how to mix mortar, and building sand vs sharp sand, and which mortar for brickwork.

Guide text, bond diagrams, the calculators, and the game graphics are original to this site. They are not AI photographs.

What the photos are for

The photos illustrate an idea: a pile of sand, a trowel on a joint, a wheelbarrow of mortar. They are not photographs of a real job, a real product, or a measured mix. Do not treat the color, texture, joint thickness, or tools in the picture as a specification or as proof that a ratio was used.

How figures are labeled

If a caption says an image is AI-generated, that is the credit. We do not present those images as documentary photos, as a brand endorsement, or as a record of work performed by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. A figure caption on an article links here so the credit sits with the picture.

If something is wrong

If a caption is missing or an image is misleading, email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com or use the contact form. General limits on the guide are in the disclaimer.
