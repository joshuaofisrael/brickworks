This policy explains what information mortarsmash.com (the "Site") handles. The short version: we collect as little as possible, we never sell your personal information, and the only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us through the contact form or by email.

Who is responsible

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC owns and operates the Site and is the data controller for it. Contact: joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups. The Site has no registration, login, comments or newsletter.
  • Contact form. Our contact form sends your name, email address and message via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to us by email. We use these details only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your details for marketing.
  • Cookieless Cloudflare analytics. The Site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first tool that does not set cookies, use local storage or fingerprint visitors. It counts page views and reports aggregate data such as page, referrer, country and browser type. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Google Analytics cookies and Google AdSense cookies from Google and other third-party advertising vendors are described below. The Site does not use social media widgets.
  • Game best score (stays on your device). The Mortar Smash game saves your best score in your browser's local storage under the name ms_best, so it is remembered next time you play. It is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing your browser's site data for mortarsmash.com.
  • Brick calculator. The numbers you enter are processed only in your browser and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Hosting logs. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. As with any website, GitHub's servers receive technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, and may log it for security and to operate the service. We do not have access to individual visitor logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Email. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply and keep a record of the conversation.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_*. Google AdSense and other third-party advertising vendors may also set cookies or use similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Those cookies are described in the sections below. The only other browser storage the Site itself sets is the game best score described above.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Contact form messages pass through FormSubmit on their way to our inbox. When advertisements are shown, Google and other third-party ad vendors may receive technical information from your browser, such as a cookie identifier and the pages you view, as described under Google AdSense. We would only disclose messages you send us if required by law.

Children

The Site is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK/EU GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, which in practice is only any message you have sent us by email or through the contact form. Email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com to make a request.
