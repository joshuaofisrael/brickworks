How thick should mortar joints be?

Plain-English mortar guidance for bricklayers and site work

Short answer

The standard joint for US modular brick is 3/8 in (9.5 mm); UK and most European brickwork uses 10 mm. Brick sizes are designed around that joint: a US modular brick of 7⅝ × 3⅝ × 2¼ in plus a 3/8 in joint gives an 8 × 4 in module and three courses per 8 in. A ½ in joint is also used with matching brick sizes.

How joint size fits brick size

BIA Technical Note 10 explains that a modular brick's nominal size equals its specified size plus the intended joint. Pick the joint, and the brick size follows.

Why sticking to the joint matters

  • Dimensions: openings, corners and courses are planned on the module. Drifting joints throw out window heads and bond.
  • Weather: full, well tooled joints are what keep rain out, and a consistent joint is easier to fill and tool evenly, see mortar joint profiles.
  • Estimating: mortar quantity depends on joint size. Our mortar calculator and brick calculator assume standard joints.

Keeping joints even

  • Use a gauge rod or storey pole marked in courses.
  • Lay corners (leads) first and run a line between them.
  • Spread mortar over only a few bricks at a time, as BIA suggests, so the bed stays plastic and the joint can be tapped down to size.

Mortar joint thickness FAQ

What is the standard mortar joint size for brick?

3/8 in (9.5 mm) for US modular brick and 10 mm in UK and European practice. Some US brick sizes are made for ½ in joints.

How many courses of brick per foot?

With US modular brick and 3/8 in joints, three courses equal 8 in, so 4½ courses per foot. In the UK, four courses of 65 mm brick with 10 mm joints equal 300 mm.

Can mortar joints be thicker than 1/2 inch?

Thicker joints are sometimes used to match old work or adjust coursing, but they are harder to fill and tool well. Follow the specification and the brick size chosen for the job.

What joint size should I use for UK bricks?

10 mm. UK brick sizes (215 × 102.5 × 65 mm) are designed so that with a 10 mm joint they work to 225 × 75 mm, keeping bond and coursing on the module.
