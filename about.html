What's here

  • The brick guide: brick history, types and sizes, bonds and patterns, how bricks are made, famous brick buildings, and care and repointing tips.
  • The brick calculator: a free tool to estimate bricks, mortar, cement and sand for a wall.
  • Mortar Smash, the game: an original brick-smashing arcade game you can play in your browser.
